5. Neural Network 🧠

Tries to think like a human brain with connected neurons.

📊 Understanding the "Report Card" Scores

Just like in school, each detective gets different grades. Let me explain each grade:

🎯 Accuracy (How often they're right overall)

Imagine: Out of 100 guesses, how many did they get right?
80% accuracy = They got 80 out of 100 correct!

🎪 ROC-AUC (The "Magic Number" - Most Important!)

Imagine: You have a magic scale from 0 to 1:
• 0.5 = As good as flipping a coin (terrible!)
• 0.7 = Pretty good detective
• 0.8 = Great detective!
• 0.9 = Almost perfect detective
• 1.0 = Perfect (never happens in real life)

🎯 Precision (When they say "sick", how often are they right?)

Imagine: If the detective points at 10 people and says "You're sick!", precision tells us how many of those 10 actually ARE sick.

🕵️ Recall (How many sick people did they actually find?)

Imagine: If there are 10 sick people in the room, recall tells us how many of those 10 the detective actually found.

⚖️ F1-Score (The balanced score)

Imagine: This is like getting both precision AND recall to work together. It's the "overall detective skill" score.
